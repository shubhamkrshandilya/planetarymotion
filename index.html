3D Solar Orrery

An interactive, procedural 3D solar system simulator modeling Newton's laws and Keplerian orbits.

Orrery Speed: 1.0x
Gravitational Constant (G): 1.5

Orbital Mechanics

F_g = -G · (M·m / r²) · r̂
T² = (4π² / G·M) · a³

Simulates gravity integration using Euler-Cromer vectors, rendering orbits directly in 3D WebGL space.

Camera: Free Orbit | Rendering: 3D WebGL Left-click and drag on the canvas to rotate the 3D viewport. Scroll to zoom.
